# Voice test data: consent form

Version `2026-10-18-v1`. A Chinese version is in [CONSENT.zh-CN.md](CONSENT.zh-CN.md). The recorder page shows the same text and stores a copy of it with your recordings.

## What this is for

Cadenza is an open-source voice typing app for macOS. We collect recordings of people reading a fixed script so we can **measure and fix recognition accuracy**. The recordings are used to test and compare speech recognition, not to train or fine-tune any model.

## What you submit

- One 16 kHz mono WAV file per sentence you record, with the reference text of that sentence.
- A random participant ID created in your browser.
- Metadata: accent or region and microphone type (required, "prefer not to say" is allowed); gender and age range (optional).

We do not ask for your name, account, or location. Nothing is uploaded by the recorder page: recordings stay in your browser until you download the zip file and send it yourself. If you submit by email, the maintainers can see your sender address; it is used only to reply, and is not stored in the dataset.

A voice is personal data. Please record alone, do not say anything private, and only take part if you are 18 or older.

## Choose one license

**A. Public (CC0).** I dedicate my recordings and metadata to the public domain under CC0 1.0. They may be published in a gated public dataset that anyone who applies can download. CC0 cannot limit what others do with the data, including training. The project itself uses it only for evaluation. Copies already downloaded cannot be recalled.

**B. Evaluation only.** My recordings are kept in a private dataset, used only by the maintainers to evaluate accuracy, not redistributed or published, and deleted when I ask.

## Deletion

Email evendstate@gmail.com with your participant ID (it is in `manifest.json` and in the zip file name). For option B we delete your files and confirm within 30 days. For option A we remove your files from the dataset and ask people with access to do the same, but we cannot guarantee that copies already downloaded are deleted.

## Taking part is voluntary

You can stop at any time. Nothing leaves your device unless you send the zip file.
