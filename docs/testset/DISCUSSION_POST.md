# Draft: GitHub Discussions announcement (English). Do not publish before the maintainer confirms.

**Title:** Help us test Cadenza's accuracy: read 90 short sentences (10–15 minutes)

Cadenza is built to be accurate on your voice, not only on ours. Right now we can only test on a few voices, so a fix that works for us may not work for you.

If you can spare 10–15 minutes, you can help:

1. Open the recorder: https://dragonkingio.github.io/cadenza-site/record/
2. Read the consent form and pick a license: **A** public (CC0, in a gated dataset) or **B** evaluation only (private, deleted on request).
3. Read the sentences aloud. You can re-record or skip any of them.
4. Download the zip file and send it as described on the last page.

**What we use it for:** measuring and fixing recognition accuracy. We do not train models on it.
**What we collect:** your recordings, a random ID, accent or region, microphone type, and optionally gender and age range. No name, account or location.
**Nothing is uploaded by the page.** The recordings stay in your browser until you send the zip file.
**Delete anytime:** email evendstate@gmail.com with your participant ID.

The script and consent form are in the repository under `docs/testset/`. You do not need a Mac to take part: any modern browser with a microphone works, and Mandarin speakers of every accent are welcome.

Questions or problems with the recorder? Reply here.
